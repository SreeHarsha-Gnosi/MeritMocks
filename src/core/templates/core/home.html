FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is the interface really the same as the IBPS exam?

The layout, status colours, buttons and section-timing rules follow the TCS iON interface used for IBPS exams. The questions are our own, written to the current pattern and difficulty.

Can I move between sections?

No — as in the real exam, each section is open only for its 20 minutes and then locks. You can submit the test once you reach the last section.

What happens if I switch tabs or exit full screen?

You get a warning, and the event is recorded. After five warnings the test is submitted automatically — practise the discipline you'll need on the day.

Do I need an account?

Not for the free mock. Accounts and the Pro pack are coming next.
